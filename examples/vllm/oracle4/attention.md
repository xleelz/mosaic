## Fix requests

### attention
- **Add `attention-backend-utils.make-local-attention-virtual-batches`** (`vllm/v1/attention/backends/utils.py:509`), plus the ChunkedLocalAttention builder that calls it (`vllm/model_executor/layers/attention/chunked_local_attention.py:56`). Input: `query_lens`, `seq_lens`, `attn_chunk_size`, `block_size` and a block table. Output: per-virtual-batch `q_seqlens`, `k_seqlens` (`seq_lens_cpu_upper_bound`) and the local block table, with `attn_chunk_size // block_size` pages per row, clipped to the last valid block. The test's six cases are the acceptance set. For example, q [4,10,5] with s [6,17,9], chunk 4 and block 2 gives q [2,2,1,4,4,1,4,1] and k [4,2,4,4,4,1,4,1]. The single case q [1], s [5] gives q [1], k [1] and block row [2,2].
