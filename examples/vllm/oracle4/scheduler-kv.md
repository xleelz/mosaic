## Fix requests

- **scheduler-kv** (`scheduler`): add the `defer_block_free` gate: `kv-transfer-config` set with `kv-role` in (kv_consumer, kv_both), and max_concurrent_batches > 1 (`async-scheduling` on the V1 runner without PP, or PP > 1). While it is on, `free-request` parks a finished or preempted request's blocks behind a fence at the newest scheduled step's sequence number. `update-from-output` releases them to the block pool once `processed_step_seq` reaches the fence (scheduler.py:1551, 1631, 2046, 2762-2790).
- **scheduler-kv** (`kv-cache-utils.get-kv-cache-config-from-groups`): for block-outermost layouts, round bytes_per_block up to `lcm(hot page sizes, per-layer block_stride_alignment)`. Accept `block_stride_alignment` per layer and a HiSparseHot marker per group, skip host-resident groups (`_pool_bytes_per_block`, kv_cache_utils.py:1239-1250), and accept every KVCacheLayout member (LBHNC, LBNHC, LHBNC, BLHNC, BLNHC, BHLNC; vllm/v1/kv_cache_layout.py:23-28).
