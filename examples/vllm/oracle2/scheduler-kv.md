## Fix requests (owning area: scheduler-kv)

1. Model `OffloadingSpecFactory.get_spec_cls` and `CPUOffloadingSpec`/`TieringOffloadingSpec` sizing as interfaces on `kv-offload` (spec.py:93-126). `cpu_page_size_per_worker` is the per-worker unpadded slot, `kv_bytes_per_chunk` is rounded up to `BLOCK_SIZE_ALIGNMENT`, and `num_chunks` is floor(cpu_bytes / aligned). Feed `shared-offload-region.init` from them.
2. Add an `encoder-cache-manager` component with state (`cache_size`, `num_free_slots`, `num_freeable_slots`, `cached`, `num_embeds_by_hash`, `request_cached_ids`, `freeable`, `freed`) and `allocate`, `free-encoder-input`, `can-allocate` and `reset`. Make `scheduler.reset-encoder-cache` call `reset`.
3. Hybrid Mamba prefix caching: support `hash_block_size < block_size` partial hits, the EAGLE drop of one hash unit, and `enable_mamba_shared_prefix_checkpoint`, which checkpoints at the junction the scheduler's `_mamba_block_aligned_split` stops at. Add the config.
